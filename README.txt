IMAGE PLACEHOLDERS

  images/about.jpg         — About / training classroom
  images/factory.jpg       — Factory floor
  images/career.jpg        — Career / office meeting

Recommended sizes:
  Hero / section photos: 1600×900 or larger (16:9 or 4:3)
  Defect photos: 1200×900 (4:3)

The site works without images (placeholders / specimen cards appear).
